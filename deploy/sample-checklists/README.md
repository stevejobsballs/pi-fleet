# Sample checklists

Checklists to start from. To use one, open pi-fleet, go to
**Equipment › Checklists**, and under *Copy a checklist from another
pi-fleet* choose the `.checklist.json` file. It opens in the builder: check
every limit against the manufacturer's service manual and your local
regulations, add the manufacturers and models it is for, then publish it
(or send it for approval).

| File | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `portable-x-ray.checklist.json` | Yearly preventive maintenance and quality control of a mobile X-ray unit with a digital detector: visual and mechanical checks, drive and emergency stops, battery, collimator alignment, kVp, time, reproducibility, linearity and half-value layer, detector QC, and IEC 62353 electrical safety. |
